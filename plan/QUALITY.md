## The five recurring causes (highest leverage first)

### C1. Manipulate starts already answered (blocking, trivial fix): 5 lessons
The watch ends with the tracer parked on the answer and the try stage keeps that
value, so the sole manipulate is solved on entry. This directly violates the
CRITIQUES rule "questions must not start pre-answered." Fix: add a final watch
beat `to` that parks the control OUTSIDE the target region.
- `inverse-graphs` (slide 3 input at 0.5 = answer pi/6)
- `trig-equations-basic` (slide 3 angle at 30 deg = a solution)
- `rational-holes` (slide 2 tracer at x=3.05, inside band 3.02..3.4, also fixes a
  "from the left it flips" narration that currently contradicts the figure)
- `rational-asymptotes` (slide 2 tracer at x=5, check only needs x>=4)
- `rational-graphing` (slide 2 tracer at x=2.1, inside band 2.02..2.4)

### C2. No manipulable control at all (blocking): 6 lessons
Watch-plus-multiple-choice only, the learner never moves the object the narration
uses. Fix: add one slider/drag that drives the existing figure, and convert one
choice item into that manipulate (predict-before-reveal).
- `mtx-3var` (all 4 slides `hideSliders`, all 12 questions choice): most severe
- `inverse-eval` (add a dial into the shaded principal-value band)
- `sum-difference-identities` (drag A and B on the circle, watch cos(A+B) update)
- `half-angle-identities` (theta slider driving theta and theta/2 on the circle)
- `verifying-identities` (a theta tracer on the IdentityGraph)
- `trig-equations-multi` (drag a tracer to each solution angle)

### C3. Quiz tests a formula the watch never teaches (Prereqs 3/4): 7 lessons
Add a short teaching beat before the quiz leans on it, or trim the item.
- `trig-equations-basic` (tangent's period pi and tan solutions, watch shows only sine)
- `sum-difference-identities` (tan(A±B) formula)
- `half-angle-identities` (tangent half-angle forms, ~9 quiz items)
- `verifying-identities` (1+tan^2=sec^2 and 1+cot^2=csc^2)
- `double-angle-identities` (tan 2theta) [SHIP, still worth closing]
- `rational-asymptotes` (Summit divides by binomials, watch shows only ÷x)
- `mtx-tx` (matrix composition NM) [SHIP, still worth closing]

### C4. AlgebraFlow header glyph scrolls off / figure squished (Visual): shared
On longer derivations the supporting glyph (unit circle, IdentityGraph, AngleCircle)
auto-scrolls out of view by watch-end, and on the tall dock the geometric figure
gets squished into a thin strip. This is a shared-layout behavior, so one fix
(pin the header, or bound the dock and move the flow into the slot as was done for
`limits-alg`) helps many at once.
- Affects: `half-angle-identities`, `verifying-identities`, `trig-equations-multi`
  (loses the unit-circle payoff), `inverse-eval`, `inverse-graphs`, and the
  squished-figure variant on `dq` (slide 3) and `continuity` (slide 2).

### C5. Plot-question A11y and marker semantics: shared
- Plot questions ("click the x-intercept/point") have no keyboard path, and the
  hit target (tolerance 0.45 ~= 36px) is under the 44px guideline. Affects
  `rational-holes`, `rational-asymptotes`, `rational-graphing` (and any future
  plot question). Fix once in the shared plot handling: add a focusable
  candidate-point list and raise tolerance to ~0.55.
- `rational-graphing` draws true x-intercepts with the open-circle **hole** marker
  (`sample-dot` CSS equals `rational-hole`), so points on the curve read as
  excluded. Fix the shared style so real intercepts are filled dots.
