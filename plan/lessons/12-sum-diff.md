# 12. Sum and difference identities

- Skill id: `sum-diff`

## Goal
Expand cosine and sine of a sum or difference with the correct sign pattern, and use it to find exact values and prove basic identities.

## Skills from the original 57-list covered here
- `sum-diff` Sum and difference identities

## Prerequisites (must already be learned)
- Unit circle exact values at 30, 45, 60, 90.
- The idea that cos(A+B) is not cos A + cos B.

## Sources to cite (>= 2 credible)
- OpenStax Precalculus 2e: Sum and Difference Identities.
- Sullivan, Precalculus: Sum and Difference Formulas.

## Slides (one idea per slide; one visual change per beat)
1. The formulas and the sign trap: cosine pairs like with like and flips the sign; sine mixes and keeps it. The middle operator is coloured to draw the eye.
2. Worked exact value: cos 75 = cos(45 + 30), expand, substitute exact values, combine to (sqrt6 - sqrt2)/4.
3. Proving a cofunction identity: cos(pi/2 - theta) expands, the zero term vanishes, leaving sin theta.

## Questions (retrieval; prefer manipulate/plot; predict-before-reveal)
- Slide 1: choice, expand cos(A+B); choice, expand sin(A-B).
- Slide 2: choice, value of cos 75; choice, correct expansion of sin(45+30).
- Slide 3: choice, sin(pi/2 - theta); choice, why the cos(pi/2) term disappears.

## Figure and interactions (draw it literally; let the learner play)
- Mini unit circle showing A, B, A+B (or 30, 45, 75, or theta and its complement).
- Each expansion writes in with a labeled arrow; signs are colour-highlighted.

## Known pitfalls to avoid
- The sign rule (cosine flips, sine keeps) is stated explicitly and repeated.
- No claim that cosine distributes over a sum.
