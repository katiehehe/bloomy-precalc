# 15. Verifying identities

- Skill id: `verify`
- Unit: Trig completion (Unit 2, block start)
- Status: authored (Journey lesson `verifying-identities`)
- Figure engine: `AlgebraFlow` (dynamic equations) + a dual-curve identity graph.

## Goal
Verify a trig identity by transforming one side into the other, working in sines and cosines and using Pythagorean and fraction moves.

## Skills from the original 57-list covered here
- `verify` Verifying identities

## Prerequisites (must already be learned)
- Reciprocal and quotient identities (sec = 1/cos, tan = sin/cos).
- Pythagorean identity sin^2 + cos^2 = 1.

## Sources to cite (>= 2 credible)
- OpenStax Precalculus 2e: Solving Trigonometric Equations with Identities (verifying identities).
- Sullivan, Precalculus: Trigonometric Identities.

## Slides (one idea per slide; one visual change per beat)
1. The rules: prove, do not solve; work one side only; convert to sine and cosine; the graph shows the two sides are one curve.
2. Worked verification of sec theta - cos theta = sin theta tan theta, transforming the right side to the left with a labeled move each line.
3. A shorter one: (1 - cos x)(1 + cos x) = sin^2 x via difference of squares and a Pythagorean swap.

## Questions (retrieval; prefer manipulate/plot; predict-before-reveal)
- Slide 1: choice, what verifying means; choice, the dependable first step.
- Slide 2: choice, rewrite tan theta; choice, which identity turns sin^2 into 1 - cos^2.
- Slide 3: choice, the product expansion; choice, 1 - cos^2 x equals what.

## Figure and interactions (draw it literally; let the learner play)
- A small graph overlays both sides; when the identity holds the dashed curve rides the solid one.
- The transformation writes itself, each line labeled with the identity used.

## Known pitfalls to avoid
- Do not move terms across the equals sign as if solving (stated as a rule).
- Only one side changes; every line is justified.
