# 13. Double-angle identities

- Skill id: `double-angle`
- Unit: Trig completion (Unit 2, block start)
- Status: authored (Journey lesson `double-angle-identities`)
- Figure engine: `AlgebraFlow` (dynamic equations) + a mini unit circle and a live numeric check.

## Goal
Derive the double-angle formulas from the sum formulas, recognize the three forms of cos 2theta, and use them.

## Skills from the original 57-list covered here
- `double-angle` Double-angle identities

## Prerequisites (must already be learned)
- Sum and difference identities (lesson 12).
- Pythagorean identity sin^2 + cos^2 = 1.

## Sources to cite (>= 2 credible)
- OpenStax Precalculus 2e: Double-Angle, Half-Angle, and Reduction Formulas.
- Sullivan, Precalculus: Double-angle and Half-angle Formulas.

## Slides (one idea per slide; one visual change per beat)
1. sin 2theta = sin(theta + theta), expand, collect the two equal terms into 2 sin theta cos theta.
2. cos 2theta = cos^2 - sin^2, then two Pythagorean swaps produce 2 cos^2 - 1 and 1 - 2 sin^2 (the three faces).
3. Interactive check: drag theta and watch sin 2theta and 2 sin theta cos theta land on the same decimal for every angle, then apply to sin theta = 3/5.

## Questions (retrieval; prefer manipulate/plot; predict-before-reveal)
- Slide 1: choice, sin 2theta; choice, where the 2 comes from.
- Slide 2: choice, which is NOT a form of cos 2theta; choice, the Pythagorean substitution result.
- Slide 3: manipulate, set theta so 2theta is a right angle (45); choice, sin 2theta from 3/5 and 4/5 (24/25).

## Figure and interactions (draw it literally; let the learner play)
- Mini unit circle with theta and 2theta; slide 3 updates both live from a theta slider.
- Slide 3 shows both sides evaluated numerically so the identity is checked, not just asserted.

## Known pitfalls to avoid
- Clarify the 2 is from adding two equal terms, not from doubling the angle.
- Manipulate starts at 20 degrees, answer is 45.
