# Product

## Register

product

## Users

High-school precalculus learners using Bloomy independently or alongside classroom instruction. They need to build an intuitive, visual model of the unit circle before practicing symbolic problems.

## Product Purpose

Bloomy turns the unit circle from a memorization diagram into something learners can manipulate, test, and understand. Base Camp should help a learner explain angle direction and conventions, connect coordinates to sine and cosine, recognize key values and quadrant signs, relate the circle to right triangles, and see how circular motion creates sinusoidal graphs.

## Brand Personality

Calm, curious, and rigorous. The voice is academic and precise, yet genuinely engaged with the mathematics: flowing, complete sentences in an expository register, closer to a strong lecturer or a well written textbook than to a chatty assistant. Enthusiasm comes from the clarity and momentum of the ideas, not from filler, exclamation, or cute asides. Every explanation is explicit and literal, stating exactly what changes and exactly why, with no vague causal language.

## Anti-references

Avoid worksheet-like pages, childish gamification, loud arcade effects, rigid slide decks, dense textbook prose, generic dashboard cards, and decoration that competes with the mathematical visual.

## Design Principles

1. Keep the mathematical object central and let explanations gather around it.
2. Ask learners to predict and manipulate before revealing an answer.
3. Introduce one meaningful visual change at a time.
4. Make progress visible without making exploration feel rushed.
5. Never trap a learner: challenges provide timely hints and a graceful way forward.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA. Support keyboard, mouse, and touch interaction; never rely on color alone; include visible focus states and meaningful labels; maintain readable contrast; and provide reduced-motion behavior for all instructional animation.
